⭐ At £11,000 a job, four a week is roughly £2.3M a year and ten a week is about £5.7M. So this is not a conversation about finding a few more enquiries. It is about more than doubling a business that already works.
🔴 What We Found, And It Backs Up What You Said
🔴🔴 There is no telephone number anywhere on your website

We read your sitemap and checked sixteen pages including your contact page. Not one of them has a clickable phone number. Not one has an email link either.

⭐⭐ You sell a £10,000 to £12,000 purchase to homeowners. A homeowner spending that wants to speak to somebody. Right now the only way to reach you from your own website is to fill in a form and wait.

⚠️ And most people read a site like yours on a phone, where tapping a number is the natural thing to do. There is nothing to tap.

🔴🔴 Every single blog post sits in a folder called "uncategorized"

Your site has 114 pages. The overwhelming majority are articles, and the address of every one of them reads:

newtechrenewablesltd.co.uk/uncategorized/is-a-heat-pump-worth-it
newtechrenewablesltd.co.uk/uncategorized/eco-4-scheme-eligibility
newtechrenewablesltd.co.uk/uncategorized/battery-storage-for-solar-panels

⭐ "Uncategorized" is the default setting in WordPress. It is what every article is filed under when nobody has set anything up. Somebody published a hundred articles and never spent the ten minutes it takes to organise them.

⚠️ That is the clearest evidence we can give you for what you already suspected. The content was produced in volume and nobody looked after it.

🔴 No accreditation appears on any page we checked

We looked for MCS, NICEIC, NAPIT, RECC, TrustMark, HIES and EPVS across ten pages including the homepage and contact page. We found none of them.

🔴 And this matters more for you than for most, because your own articles sell ECO4, the Boiler Upgrade Scheme and battery storage. Those schemes are tied to certification. A homeowner who has read about ECO4 will look for proof you can actually deliver it.

⚠️ We are not saying you do not hold these. You almost certainly do, or you could not do the work. We are saying a customer cannot see it, and in solar that is one of the two or three things people check before they ring anybody.

🔴 No advertising tracking of any kind

You have Google Analytics and nothing else. No Google Ads tag, no Meta pixel, no conversion tracking.

⚠️ Which tells us something about the agency. If anybody had run paid advertising properly, a tag would be sitting on the site. Either nothing was ever run, or it was run without anybody measuring what came back.

⛔ And it means nobody can tell you which of those 114 pages has ever produced a customer.

⚠️ No local business details in the code

No address, postcode, opening hours or map coordinates where search engines read them. For a Glasgow installer competing on local searches, that is a gap, and it is an hour or two of work.

⭐ What Is Genuinely Good, Because A Lot Is
⭐ Somebody did real work here. It is just unfinished
  • 114 pages. That is a serious content investment and most installers have six pages and a gallery.
  • Articles aimed at real questions — ECO4 eligibility, heat pump running costs, is battery storage worth it, best heating for an old house. ⭐ That is exactly what a homeowner types into Google before they buy.
  • Solar, battery storage and heat pumps all covered. Three products, not one.
  • Finance mentioned, which matters at your price point.
  • A team page and reviews.
  • A form on every page, including the articles. ⭐ Whoever built it got that part right.
  • Google Analytics installed, so there is at least a record of traffic.

⭐⭐ So the raw material is there and it was not cheap. What is missing is the handful of things that turn a reader into a phone call. That is a much better position than having nothing.

The Size Of What You Are Asking For

Four a week to ten a week, at your own numbers

6 more clients a week

At £11,000 a job that is about £66,000 a week of additional revenue, or roughly £3.4M a year on top of where you are. Going from around £2.3M to around £5.7M.

⚠️ Midpoint of the £10,000 to £12,000 range you gave us, multiplied out. Not a forecast.

⚠️ And the question that has to come first: could you install ten a week? ⭐ Six more installs a week is not a marketing problem, it is a business one — crews, surveyors, scaffolding, and materials. If the honest answer is that you could not, we would rather know tomorrow than three months in.
On The Agency That Went Badly
You said some good, some bad. Here is what the site suggests happened

⭐ The good part is visible. Somebody produced a lot of genuinely sensible content aimed at real buyer questions. That takes time and money and it is still there, working for you.

🔴 The bad part is also visible, and it is all the same kind of thing:

  • No phone number on any page
  • Every article filed under the default category
  • No accreditation shown anywhere
  • No advertising tracking, so nothing was measured
  • No local business details in the code

⭐⭐ Every one of those is a finishing job, not a building job. Which is consistent with what you described: somebody who delivered volume and then stopped paying attention.

You said you need somebody to control the marketing side. ⭐ What that actually means here is somebody who finishes things and then measures whether they worked. Those two habits would have prevented every finding on this page.

What We Would Want To Ask
  • 1. Could you install ten a week? ⭐ The first question, and everything else depends on it. Crews, surveyors, materials.
  • 2. Where do your four a week come from now? Referrals, the website, a lead supplier, door to door?
  • 3. Do you hold MCS? And RECC, HIES or TrustMark? ⚠️ If you do, none of it is on your website, and that is a quick fix worth more than it sounds.
  • 4. Has anyone ever run paid advertising for you? There is no tag on the site, which usually means no. If somebody did, we would want to know what happened.
  • 5. What did the agency actually do, and what did it cost? ⭐ Not to criticise them. To work out what is already paid for and still useful.
  • 6. Who wrote the 114 articles, and do you still have access to whoever did?
  • 7. What does a customer do when they want to speak to somebody? ⚠️ Genuinely curious, because the website gives them no way to.
  • 8. What does "controlling the marketing" look like to you? Reports, a weekly call, somebody you can ring? ⭐ Being burned once usually means the answer is specific.
Question 1 decides whether there is a deal at all. ⭐ Question 7 is the one most likely to make him stop and think, because he will not have realised.
